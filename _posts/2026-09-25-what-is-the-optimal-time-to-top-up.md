# what is the optimal<sup>*</sup> time to top up?

What are the most advantageous times for activating a new card and taking full advantage of promotional top-up offers?

## Activating a new card

The best time to activate a new card is during the Mid-Autumn Festival, National Day, or May Day Labor Day.

| time                                         | Chinese              | rating | recommended                                           |
|----------------------------------------------|----------------------|--------|-------------------------------------------------------|
| National Day                                 | 国庆节               | 5/5    | promotional offers, approaching `Mid-Autumn Festival` |
| Mid-Autumn Festival                          | 中秋节               | 5/5    | promotional offers, approaching `National Day`        |
| May Day Labor Day                            | 五一劳动节           | 4.5/5  | promotional offers                                    |
| back-to-school season (usually in September) | 开学季（通常在九月） | 5/5    | university enrollment<sup>*</sup> time                |

An additional factor to consider is the city where the opening branch of the bank is located.

| city                                    | rating | recommended                                      | example         |
|-----------------------------------------|--------|--------------------------------------------------|-----------------|
| Shanghai                                | 5/5    | Financial city in China                          | -               |
| Beijing                                 | 4/5    | Capital of China                                 | -               |
| Guangzhou                               | 4/5    | Top city in China                                | -               |
| Shenzhen                                | 4.5/5  | special economic zone city, closing to Hong Kong | -               |
| the provincial capital of your province | 3.5/5  | easy to access                                   | Wuhan, Hangzhou |
| a major city in your province           | 3.2/5  | easy to access                                   | Ningbo, Xiamen  |
