## Credit / Debit Card

### UnionPay

Why I advise against applying for Visa / Mastercard cards issued by most Chinese banks?

Including Visa, Mastercard, and American Express, most of the credit and debit cards issued by most Chinese banks are invalid at card readers when used abroad.

There are so many banks available in China that it can be difficult for you to choose.

I advise you **not** to apply for a dual-standard UnionPay credit card, as there are many restrictions on discounts and benefits during promotional activities.

### Mastercard

CIB universal life Mastercard credit card with the symbol "Business executive" on the cover can't be successfully added to Apple Pay.

HSBC HK Mastercard debit card and BOC HK Mastercard debit card, issued by banks in Hong Kong, are good choices and can be successfully added to Apple Pay.

### Visa

Visa cards are more common than American Express cards, but not as common as Mastercard.

I have two Visa debit cards, both converted from virtual cards issued by digital banks in Hong Kong.

I don't have a Visa card issued by a bank in China.

### American Express

American Express cards are rarely seen in our daily lives, while UnionPay cards are the most common in China.
