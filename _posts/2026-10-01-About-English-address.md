## Example

To check the postal code of address, please refer to the [China Post website](https://dey.11185.cn/web/#/idtoolkitaddress)

```text
Mr Liu Wei
1298 Yong An Road
100050 BEIJING
CHINA
```

```text
Mr Liu Bao
TIANHE QU, XINTANG JIEDAO, LINGTANG XIAJIE NANXIANG 21 HAO 201 FANG
Guangzhou, Guangdong, 510640
China
```

> [!TIP]
> __*__:
>
> - boulevard: A `boulevard` is a wide street in a city, usually with trees along each side. 林荫大道
> - designator: 标识符;指定者 designate: [Verb] when you `designate` someone or something `as` a particular thing, you formally give them that description or name. 标示;把...定名为;称呼<br/>
>   [Verb] if something `is designated for` a particular purpose, it is set aside for that purpose. 指定<br/>
>   [Verb] when you `designate` someone `as` something, you formally choose them to do that particular job. 指派;选派;委任<br/>
>   [Adj.] `designate` is used to describe someone who has been formally chosen to do a particular job, but has not yet started doing it. (已受委派)尚未上任的;(已当选)尚未就职的
