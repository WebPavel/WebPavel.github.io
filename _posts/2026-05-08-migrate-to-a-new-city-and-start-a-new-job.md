# migrate to a new city and start a new job

Migrate to a new city and start a new job. What do you need to prepare?

## residence permit

The most important thing to do first is to apply for a new residence permit from the local police station when you settle in a new place.

Today I learned that in some places, like Beijing, residence permits can be classified into two types.
- General residence permits are assigned to those who live in the city.
- Work residence permits are assigned to people who have a legal job in the city.

## Housing

Renting a new house provides the foundation for a living experience for a long time to come.

Is the rented house far from your workplace?

Is it far from shopping malls?

Is it far from top-tier 3A hospitals<sup>*</sup>?

Are there any parks nearby?

Are there any universities or colleges nearby?

Are there any barbershops nearby?

Are there any pharmacies<sup>*</sup> nearby?

Are there any laundromats<sup>*</sup> nearby, preferably 24-hour self-service?

Are there any hardware<sup>*</sup> stores nearby?

Are there any telecommunications service providers nearby?

Are there any libraries nearby (optional)?

Are there any sports hall nearby?

## Transport

Is there Metro/MRT(Mass Rapid Transit)/MTR(Mass Transit Railway, only available in Hong Kong) station nearby?

Is the rented house far from the Metro/MRT(Mass Rapid Transit)/MTR(Mass Transit Railway, only available in Hong Kong) station?

Is it close to major public transportation hubs, such as railway stations, highways, expressways?

Are there any bus stops nearby (optional)?

## Eating

Find some delicacies near the rented house.

- breakfast: rice balls, sticky rice chicken, steamed buns, tea eggs, hot dry noodles, breakfast shops, bread shops.
- lunch/dinner:
  - Cantonese cuisine, Hunan spicy stir-fry, Hainan coconut chicken, hot pot, buffet, barbecue, Korean BBQ
  - old friend rice noodles, Chongqing noodles, shacha noodles, biangbiang noodles, dumplings.
  - fast-food restaurants, pizza, hamburger.
- cake shops, 24-hour convenience stores, ice cream shops, bubble tea shops, coffee shops.
- branded supermarket:
  - Fresh hippo (盒马)<sup>*</sup>
  - Carrefour
  - PX Mart (大全聯)(formerly RT-Mart 大潤發 in Taiwan in 2025)

>   - hippo: hippopotamus, 源自希腊語河馬，English 縮写成 'hippo', a very large African animal with short legs and thick, hairless skin, live in and near rivers.
